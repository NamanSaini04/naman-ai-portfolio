## Audit v6 responsive thumbnails

Full-size links still open the original PNGs above, whose bytes and hashes are unchanged. Inline images now use resized WebP derivatives encoded with lossless WebP compression (Pillow, Lanczos resampling, `lossless=True`, `method=6`). Resizing is the only visual transformation; no labels, data, colours, or annotations were edited. The PNGs remain authoritative for reading fine chart/diagram text.

- Fraud: 440 × 220 and 880 × 440, from the original 880 × 440 PNG.
- SOC: 645 × 354 and 1290 × 708, from the original 1935 × 1062 PNG.

These dimensions preserve the exact original aspect ratios. Explicit width/height, responsive `srcset`/`sizes`, lazy loading, captions, alt text, and full-size links are retained. No originals are fetched for the inline image; opening a full-size link loads its PNG separately. Derivatives live in `src/assets/projects/thumbnails/`.
